Route and Travel Time

All Braj towns are reached via the Agra–Mathura highway. Gokul lies just southeast of Mathura across the Yamuna, about 1.5–2 hours from Agra. Nandgaon and Barsana sit further north-west of Mathura, roughly another hour beyond — the three villages form a natural arc that a private cab covers comfortably in a day, something very hard to do by public transport.

We pick up from any Agra address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt, or Kheria Airport.

Places Covered

Gokul

The village where Lord Krishna spent his infancy with Nanda Baba and Yashoda after his birth in Mathura. Key spots include Nand Bhavan (Chaurasi Khamba) and the Yamuna ghats.

Nandgaon

Nanda Baba's hilltop village, crowned by the Nand Bhavan temple with sweeping views over Braj. Expect some stairs to the temple — the driver drops you at the closest permitted point.

Barsana (add-on)

Radha Rani's village and its famous hilltop Shriji Temple — pairs naturally with Nandgaon, which sits just a few kilometres away.

Mathura (add-on)

Krishna Janmabhoomi and Dwarkadhish Temple — easy to include since every Braj route passes through Mathura.

Vrindavan (add-on)

Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir, ISKCON — add it if your day allows, or keep it for a separate unhurried visit.
