# Google review playbook

How Padma Shree Travels asks for Google reviews and replies to them. For the owner and anyone who messages customers after a trip.

The review page is `https://www.padmashreetravels.in/review-helper/`. It does **not** give customers a ready-made review. They pick their trip, see a few questions to jog their memory, can tap place names (Amber Fort, Prem Mandir, Buland Darwaza…), tap short facts that were true for their trip (on time, clean car, fare as quoted), speak or type a line in their own words, then copy it into Google. They choose their own star rating on Google.

---

## The rules (Google policy and Indian consumer rules on fake reviews)

Breaking these can get reviews removed in bulk or the Google profile suspended, which costs far more ranking than a few missing reviews.

1. **Ask every customer, after every trip.** Don't only ask the happy ones. Asking selectively is called review gating and is not allowed.
2. **Never write the review for them**, suggest wording, or send them "sample" text to paste.
3. **Never ask for 5 stars.** Ask for an honest review.
4. **Never offer anything for a review**: no discount, cashback, free ride or gift.
5. **Never post reviews yourself**, or from staff, family or driver accounts.
6. **The customer posts from their own phone and Google account.** Drivers must not take the customer's phone and post it for them, or hand over a phone at drop-off.
7. **One reminder at most**, two or three days later. Then leave it.

---

## When and how to send

- Send the WhatsApp message the same evening, or the next morning for late trips.
- Use the link for the trip they actually took. It opens the page with that trip already selected.
- For customers who chatted in Hindi, send the Hindi message. Its link opens the page in Hindi (`&lang=hi`).
- Replace `{name}` with the customer's name. Keep the rest short.

## Replying to reviews

Reply to **every** review, good or bad, from the business's Google profile, within **24–48 hours**. Replies are public. Future customers read them as closely as the reviews.

- **Thank them by name.**
- **Mention the trip they actually took**, in plain words ("the drive to Jaipur", "your parents' darshan in Vrindavan"). That's natural and useful to readers.
- **Don't stuff keywords.** Write "glad the Jaipur drive went well", not "best Agra to Jaipur taxi service in Agra".
- **Don't copy-paste the same reply.** Readers notice, and so does Google.
- **Complaints:** stay calm, don't argue in public, say sorry for what went wrong, and say what you've actually done about it. Only promise changes you have really made. Move the details to a phone call.
- **Never reveal** booking details, phone numbers or anything the customer didn't write themselves.
- Reply in the language they wrote in.

### Example replies

Use these to get the tone right. Rewrite them each time, don't paste them.

**1. Happy review, Jaipur**

> Thank you, {name} ji. Glad the drive to Jaipur went smoothly and the stop at Fatehpur Sikri worked out. We'll pass your kind words on to {driver}. Safe travels, and do call us whenever you're back in Agra.

**2. Happy review, Mathura–Vrindavan with elderly parents**

> Radhe Radhe, {name} ji. Thank you for taking the time to write this. We're really glad your parents could do Banke Bihari and Prem Mandir without being rushed. That's exactly how a darshan day should feel.

**3. Short review, Fatehpur Sikri**

> Thanks, {name}! Happy you enjoyed Fatehpur Sikri. Buland Darwaza is something else in person. Hope the rest of your Agra trip goes well.

**4. Stars only, no text**

> Thank you for the rating, {name} ji. Hope to drive you again soon.

**5. Mixed review (late pickup)**

> {name} ji, thank you for the honest feedback, and we're sorry the pickup was late. That shouldn't happen, especially before an early start. We now confirm every early-morning pickup with the driver the night before. Glad the rest of the day went well.

*Only write the "we now…" part if you have actually made that change.*

**6. Unhappy review (disagreement about the fare)**

> {name} ji, we're sorry the trip ended on a bad note. We should have made the tolls and parking extras clearer before you set off. Please call us on +91 87200 81102 so we can talk it through and put it right.

**7. Review in Hindi**

> धन्यवाद {name} जी। खुशी है कि मथुरा–वृंदावन की यात्रा आपको अच्छी लगी और दर्शन के समय ड्राइवर ने धैर्य रखा। आपका परिवार फिर से आए, यही कामना है। राधे राधे!

**8. Airport / station pickup**

> Thank you, {name}. Glad the driver was waiting when you came out at Agra Cantt and helped with the bags. Have a good journey home.

## Tracking (optional)

The review page sends three events to Google Tag Manager (container `GTM-NWDQLKM9`). They contain the trip type and the review length, never the review text:

| Event | When | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `review_trip_selected` | Customer taps a trip | `trip` |
| `review_copy_open` | Customer copies their review and Google opens | `trip`, `chars` |
| `review_open_google` | Google opens without copying (empty box, "Open Google again", or the "Open Google Review" button) | `trip` |

To see them in GA4, add a Custom Event trigger for each in GTM and forward it with a GA4 Event tag. Until then they only sit in the page's `dataLayer`.
