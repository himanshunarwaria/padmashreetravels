# Tourist Pitch Messaging Kit

Created 31 August 2026, Part D of the tourist-acquisition phase (page-upgrade-log
entry #40). This is a **reference document only** - no message below has been
sent to anyone, and no partner, hotel, guide or customer has been contacted as
part of producing it. Every message is a ready-to-use template for a human at
Padma Shree Travels to personalise and send when they choose to.

## Rules every message here follows

* No "best," "cheapest," "safest," "No. 1," or similar superlative/comparative
  claims - none can be verified, and several are exactly what
  `docs/claims-policy-audit.md` already flagged and removed from the live site.
* No guarantees ("guaranteed availability," "100% safe," "never late"). Where a
  real commitment exists (fare in writing, driver details before pickup), the
  message states that specific commitment instead of a generic promise.
* Every fact used (fares, phone number, policies) is one already published and
  verified elsewhere on this site - nothing here introduces a new claim.
* WhatsApp-bound messages are kept short enough to read on a phone screen
  without scrolling past a screenful of text.
* `[bracketed]` fields are placeholders for the sender to fill in - never send
  a message with a placeholder still in it.

## 1. International visitor

**When to use:** a foreign-passport enquiry, or a guest clearly asking from
outside India (currency questions, "is this safe for tourists" phrasing).

> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. Padma Shree Travels runs private AC
> cabs in Agra with the fare confirmed in writing before you travel - no
> meter, no bargaining. Your driver's name, phone number and car number are
> shared before pickup. Tolls, parking and monument tickets are separate and
> we tell you what to expect upfront. What are your travel dates and which
> route or trip are you planning?

## 2. Domestic leisure traveller

**When to use:** an Indian traveller planning a personal trip (not
pilgrimage-specific), asking about sightseeing or an outstation route.

> Hi [Name], for [route/trip], the fare is confirmed on WhatsApp before you
> travel and your driver's details are shared beforehand. Let me know your
> date, number of passengers, and preferred cab type (sedan, Ertiga or
> Innova) and I'll share the fare.

## 3. Family

**When to use:** a group booking mentioning children, elders, or multiple
generations travelling together.

> Hi [Name], for a family group we'd suggest [Ertiga/Innova] for more room -
> happy to confirm which fits your numbers and luggage. The driver waits at
> each stop within your booked day, so there's no rush between monuments or
> temples. Let me know your dates and group size and I'll share the fare.

## 4. Senior citizen

**When to use:** a booking mentioning elderly parents/grandparents, or a
question about comfort, walking distance, or pace.

> Hi [Name], for a trip with elders we usually recommend the Innova Crysta
> for easier boarding and a softer ride, and a day plan with fewer stops and
> more time at each. Some routes involve walking from parking to the temple
> or monument gate - happy to tell you honestly what a specific route
> involves before you book. What are you planning?

## 5. Pilgrimage traveller

**When to use:** an enquiry about Mathura, Vrindavan, Mehandipur Balaji,
Kaila Devi, or a temple circuit.

> Hi [Name], for [temple/route], the driver waits during darshan at no extra
> charge within your booked day. Fare is confirmed on WhatsApp before you
> travel. Let me know your date, group size and whether you'd like a
> same-day or two-day plan, and I'll share the details.

## 6. Golden Triangle traveller

**When to use:** an enquiry mentioning Delhi, Agra and Jaipur together, or
"Golden Triangle" by name.

> Hi [Name], we're based in Agra and can help with the Agra-Delhi and
> Agra-Jaipur legs of your trip, in either direction, plus Agra sightseeing
> in between. We don't have a fixed all-in package - each leg is quoted on
> its own. Tell me your direction and dates and I'll say clearly what we can
> cover and the fare for each leg.

## 7. Airport passenger

**When to use:** an enquiry mentioning a flight, Kheria Airport, or IGI
Delhi.

> Hi [Name], for your airport transfer, share your flight number and arrival
> or departure time and we'll track it - pickup adjusts automatically if the
> flight is delayed, no extra charge within a reasonable window. Driver name,
> phone number and car number are shared before you land. What's your flight
> number and drop location?

## 8. Railway passenger

**When to use:** an enquiry mentioning Agra Cantt, a train number, or
arrival/departure by rail.

> Hi [Name], share your train number and arrival time and your driver will
> be waiting at the station exit when you arrive - adjusted if the train
> runs late. Driver details are shared beforehand. Are you continuing
> straight to sightseeing, or just need the transfer?

## 9. Hotel reception

**When to use:** introducing the referral arrangement to a hotel front desk
for the first time (see `partners/index.html` for the full page this links
to). This is an outreach message from Padma Shree Travels, not a message a
hotel sends onward.

> Hi, I'm reaching out from Padma Shree Travels, a taxi service based in
> Agra. We're introducing a small referral arrangement for a few local
> hotels - guests get a fixed fare confirmed in writing and driver details
> before pickup, and your desk doesn't need to manage the booking itself.
> No terms are fixed until we've spoken about what would work for your
> property. Would you be open to a short call?

## 10. Homestay

**When to use:** the same outreach as #9, adapted for a smaller, more
personal property where the owner is likely the point of contact directly.

> Hi [Name], I run Padma Shree Travels, a taxi service in Agra. A few of our
> guests-in-common have asked their homestay hosts about onward travel, so
> I wanted to introduce ourselves directly - fixed fare in writing, driver
> details shared before pickup, no hidden extras. Happy to talk through
> whether a simple referral arrangement would suit your guests, with terms
> agreed individually. No obligation either way.

## 11. Tour guide

**When to use:** outreach to an independent, licensed tour guide who may
refer clients needing transport (not a guide Padma Shree Travels employs -
see `agra-local-sightseeing/`'s own FAQ, which states a guide is arranged
separately and at extra cost, never bundled by default).

> Hi [Name], I'm with Padma Shree Travels, a private taxi service in Agra.
> When your clients need a car for the day or an onward transfer, we
> confirm the fare in writing before travel and share driver details
> beforehand, so there's nothing for you to manage on the day. Would it be
> useful to stay in touch for when a client needs a cab?

## 12. Post-trip review request

**When to use:** sent after a completed trip, once the driver has confirmed
drop-off.

> Hi [Name], hope the trip went smoothly today. If you have a minute, a
> genuine review helps other travellers find us:
> https://g.page/r/CUjtXu1x_3bPEBM - only if you'd like to, no pressure
> either way. Thank you for travelling with us.

## 13. Repeat customer

**When to use:** re-engaging someone who has booked before, for a new trip
or a seasonal reminder (festival season, a new route they haven't tried).

> Hi [Name], good to hear from you again. Same as before - fare confirmed
> on WhatsApp before you travel, driver details shared beforehand. What's
> the plan this time - same route, or somewhere new?

## 14. Referral request

**When to use:** asking a satisfied past customer if they'd mention Padma
Shree Travels to someone they know - not a paid or incentivised referral
programme unless the owner decides to create one (see the open items in
`partners/index.html`).

> Hi [Name], glad the trip worked out well. If a friend or family member is
> ever visiting Agra and needs a cab, feel free to pass on our number:
> +91-87200 81102. No obligation - just wanted to mention it since you've
> travelled with us before.
