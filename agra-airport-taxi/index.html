Agra Airport Taxi — Questions & Answers

Where is Agra airport and what is it called?

Agra’s airport is Kheria Airport, also known as Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyay Airport (IATA code AGR), located a few kilometres from the city centre and shared with an Indian Air Force base.

What is the Agra airport taxi fare?

Airport transfers within the city start at ₹800 for an AC sedan. The exact fare depends on your drop location — message us your hotel or address on WhatsApp for a fixed quote.

Do you track my flight for delays?

Yes. Share your flight number when booking and we keep track of the schedule, adjusting the pickup time if your flight is delayed — no extra charge for waiting within a reasonable window.

Can I combine airport pickup with Agra sightseeing?

Yes. Many travellers combine their airport pickup with a full-day Agra sightseeing tour covering the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and other monuments, at one fixed combined price. Ask us on WhatsApp when booking.

How will I recognise my driver at the airport?

Your driver’s name, phone number and car registration number are shared with you on WhatsApp before your flight lands, along with a name-board pickup at arrivals.

Is airport pickup available at night or early morning?

Yes. Tell us your flight time — including late-night arrivals and early-morning departures — and we confirm availability in writing before you book.
