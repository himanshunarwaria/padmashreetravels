Route and Travel Time

Govardhan lies in the Braj region of Uttar Pradesh, about 85–95 km from Agra via Mathura. Your cab takes the Agra–Mathura highway, then continues towards Govardhan — expect around 2 hours of driving each way, depending on traffic near Mathura.

We pick up from any Agra address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt, or Kheria Airport.

Govardhan Parikrama Travel Note

The Govardhan Parikrama is a sacred walking circuit of roughly 21–23 km around Govardhan Hill, and it is completed on foot (or, for some, by rolling prostration) — the car cannot follow the path itself. Your driver drops you at the parikrama start point, waits at an agreed spot or nearby parking, and picks you up once you're done. Tell us your plan on WhatsApp so we can time the pickup properly, since a full Parikrama can take several hours depending on your pace.

Places Covered

Govardhan Hill

The sacred hill at the centre of the Govardhan Parikrama, revered as Lord Krishna's lifted mountain. Several temples line the base of the hill.

Radha Kund

A pair of sacred ponds associated with Radha and Krishna, a short drive from Govardhan and a peaceful stop for many pilgrims.

Kusum Sarovar

A beautifully carved sandstone ghat and cenotaph complex near Govardhan, popular for its architecture and quiet atmosphere.

Gokul, Barsana & Mathura (optional add-ons)

If your schedule allows, these nearby Braj towns can be added to make a fuller day of darshan. Tell us what you'd like to cover and we'll plan the route.
