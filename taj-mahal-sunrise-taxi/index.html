Why Visit the Taj Mahal at Sunrise?

The early morning light gives the white marble a soft pink-gold glow that photographs beautifully, the temperature is far more comfortable than midday, and the crowds are noticeably thinner than any other time of day. Gates open around sunrise, which shifts through the year — your driver will confirm the exact recommended pickup time for your travel date.
