Why Visit The Taj Mahal At Sunrise?

The early morning light gives the white marble a soft pink-gold glow that photographs beautifully, the temperature is far more comfortable than midday, and the crowds are noticeably thinner than any other time of day. Gate-opening time shifts through the year with the actual sunrise — your driver confirms the exact recommended pickup for your travel date rather than a fixed clock time.

Taj Mahal Sunrise Taxi — Questions & Answers

What time should we leave for Taj Mahal sunrise?

We recommend a 5:00–5:30 AM pickup from your hotel, depending on the season and your hotel’s distance from the East Gate, so you are in the queue before the gate opens at sunrise.

What is the fare for a Taj Mahal sunrise taxi?

The sunrise trip is priced the same as the half-day Taj Mahal package — from ₹1,500 for an AC sedan, covering the Taj Mahal visit with the option to add Agra Fort afterward.

Why is sunrise the best time to visit the Taj Mahal?

The early morning light gives the white marble a soft pink-gold glow, the temperature is cooler, and the crowds are noticeably thinner than mid-morning or afternoon.

Which gate is best for a sunrise visit?

The East Gate is generally preferred for sunrise visits because of shorter queues at opening time. Your driver will confirm the best gate based on the day.

Is the Taj Mahal closed on Fridays for a sunrise visit too?

Yes. The Taj Mahal is closed to sightseeing visitors every Friday, regardless of what time you arrive, open only to those attending prayers at the mosque inside the complex. Plan your sunrise visit on another day of the week.

Can we add Mehtab Bagh for a sunset view too?

Yes. Mehtab Bagh, across the Yamuna river, offers a classic view of the Taj Mahal and is popular at sunset. Ask us to add it to your itinerary — many guests combine a sunrise Taj visit with a sunset Mehtab Bagh stop later the same day.

Will the driver wait during the visit?

Yes. Your driver waits at the parking area for the full duration of your visit at no extra charge, and can continue on to Agra Fort or your hotel afterward.
