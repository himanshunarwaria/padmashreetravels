Route and Travel Time

Fatehpur Sikri sits about 40 km west of Agra, a comfortable one-hour drive via NH-19 or the Fatehpur Sikri Road. The road is in good condition throughout, making this one of the easiest half-day trips from Agra. Most travellers leave by mid-morning, spend 2–3 hours exploring the complex, and are back in Agra well before evening.

We pick up from any address in Agra — your hotel, Agra Cantt railway station, Agra Fort station, or Kheria Airport. The trip pairs naturally with a full Agra sightseeing day; tell us on WhatsApp if you'd like to combine both.
