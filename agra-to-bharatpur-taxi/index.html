What The Day Covers

Keoladeo National Park

The Bharatpur bird sanctuary, a UNESCO World Heritage wetland. Flat walking paths and cycle-rickshaws are both available inside, so you can do as much or as little on foot as you want. Your driver takes you to the entrance and points out the ticket counter.

Lohagarh Fort — optional

Bharatpur’s “Iron Fort”, known for having withstood several sieges. Worth the stop if you have time left in town after the park.

Entry tickets are separate

Park entry is bought at the gate and is not part of the taxi fare. We say so here rather than letting it surprise you at the counter.

Agra To Bharatpur — Questions & Answers

How far is Bharatpur from Agra?

Bharatpur is approximately 55 km from Agra via NH-21, about 1 to 1.5 hours by private taxi on a well-maintained road. It is the shortest outstation day trip we run.

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Bharatpur?

We do not publish a fixed starting price for this route. A same-day round trip is available in an AC sedan, with Ertiga and Innova Crysta options for families and groups. Tolls, park entry tickets and parking are extra at actuals and told to you before booking. Message us on WhatsApp for today’s fixed fare.

What is the best time to visit Keoladeo National Park?

October to February is generally considered the better window for birding, when migratory birds are more commonly seen. Sightings depend on the season, the weather and simple luck on the day, so we cannot guarantee specific birds or numbers — please treat this as general seasonal guidance rather than a promise. What we can promise is that the driver knows the park gate timings and where to drop you.

Do we need separate tickets for Keoladeo National Park?

Yes. Entry tickets for Keoladeo National Park are bought at the gate and are not included in the taxi fare. Your driver can point you to the ticket counter on arrival.

Can we combine Bharatpur with Fatehpur Sikri or Jaipur?

Yes. Fatehpur Sikri lies on this same road between Agra and Bharatpur, about 40 km from Agra, so it is an easy add-on in either direction. The road also continues to Jaipur if you are travelling on rather than returning. Tell us your plan on WhatsApp and we will quote one fixed fare for the full route.

Is the trip suitable for families and senior citizens?

Yes, and it is one of the easier trips we run. Keoladeo has flat walking paths, and cycle-rickshaws are available inside for anyone who would rather not walk the whole way. Ask your driver for guidance at the gate. The drive itself is only about an hour and a quarter each way.

Will the driver wait while we are in the park?

Yes. Waiting is included within the booked day, so there is no charge for however long you spend inside, and your luggage stays locked in the car while you are in the park.
