Route and Travel Time

Bharatpur lies about 55 km from Agra via NH21, on the same road that continues towards Fatehpur Sikri and Jaipur. The drive takes about 1 to 1.5 hours each way on a well-maintained highway, making it one of the easiest outstation day trips from Agra.

We pick up from any Agra address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt, or Kheria Airport.

Best Season Note

October to February is generally considered the better window for birding at Keoladeo National Park, when a wider range of migratory birds tends to be present. That said, sightings depend on season, weather, and simple luck on the day — we can't guarantee specific birds or numbers, so please treat this as general seasonal guidance rather than a promise.

Places Covered

Keoladeo National Park (Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary)

A UNESCO World Heritage wetland known for its birdlife. Cycle-rickshaws and walking paths are both available inside — your driver can guide you to the entrance and ticket counter.

Lohagarh Fort (optional)

Bharatpur's historic "Iron Fort," known for having withstood several sieges. A worthwhile stop if you have extra time in town.

Fatehpur Sikri (optional en route)

The Mughal ghost city lies between Agra and Bharatpur, making it an easy add-on stop either way. Ask your driver to plan the halt.
