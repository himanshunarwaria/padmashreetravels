Outstation Cabs From Agra — Questions & Answers

Which outstation routes do you cover from Agra?

We run regular outstation routes to Jaipur, Gwalior, Bharatpur, Aligarh, Etawah, Firozabad, Hathras, Tundla, Sirsaganj, Shikohabad and Bateshwar, plus Agra Airport, Agra railway station and Delhi Airport transfers. If your destination is not listed, message us on WhatsApp — we quote custom routes too.

Is one-way taxi available, or only round trip?

Both are available on most routes. Tell us your plan when booking and we confirm the appropriate fixed fare — one-way drop, round trip, or a same-day return.

How is the outstation fare calculated?

The fare depends on the distance, route and cab type. We confirm a fixed fare on WhatsApp before you travel — it covers fuel and driver charges, with tolls and parking paid by you at actuals and told upfront.

Can I book a custom outstation route not listed on your site?

Yes. Message us your destination, travel date and passenger count on WhatsApp, and we plan the route and quote a fixed fare, the same way we do for our listed routes.

Which cab should I choose for an outstation trip?

An AC sedan suits up to 4 passengers with regular luggage. An AC SUV such as an Ertiga or Innova Crysta suits up to 6 or 7 passengers, or a smaller group with more luggage. A Tempo Traveller is available for larger groups of 12 to 15. Tell us your group size on WhatsApp and we recommend the right vehicle.

Do you provide driver details before pickup for outstation trips?

Yes. For every outstation booking, your driver’s name, mobile number and car registration number are shared with you on WhatsApp before pickup.
