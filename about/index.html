About Padma Shree Travels

A local taxi and cab booking service based in Shamsabad, Agra — here's who we are, where we work, and how booking with us works.

Who We Are

Padma Shree Travels is a local taxi and cab booking service based in Shamsabad, Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. We serve tourists, pilgrims, families, and business travellers with fixed-fare, air-conditioned private cabs driven by experienced local drivers who know the city, the temple routes, and the highways in and out of Agra.

Where We're Based

Our office is at Rajeshwar Mandir, Kaveri Vihar Phase II, Shamsabad, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282001. Pickups are arranged from anywhere in Agra — your hotel, home, Agra Cantt railway station, Agra Fort station, or Kheria Airport — we come to you.

Routes We Serve

Our core routes are:

  • Agra sightseeing and Taj Mahal visits — full-day sightseeing, Taj Mahal taxi, and sunrise visits
  • Braj pilgrimage tours — Mathura, Vrindavan, and Barsana, with custom extensions like Govardhan available on request
  • Rajasthan temple darshan — Mehandipur Balaji Temple and Kaila Devi Temple near Karauli, including our combined two-temple tour
  • Agra–Jaipur transfers — one way and round trip, via the Agra to Jaipur and Jaipur to Agra routes
  • Airport and railway station pickups — from Kheria Airport and Agra Cantt railway station
  • Outstation trips across Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan — see our full outstation cabs page

Browse the complete list of pilgrimage routes on our temple tours hub.

How Customers Book

Booking is by phone or WhatsApp — no app to download, no account to create.

  1. Message or call us with your route, travel date, and pickup point.
  2. We confirm your fare in writing on WhatsApp before you travel, including expected tolls and parking.
  3. Your driver's name, phone number, and vehicle number are shared before every pickup — match the number plate before you board.

No advance payment is required for most bookings.

What Makes Us Trustworthy

The fare we confirm on WhatsApp is the fare you pay. Tolls and parking are charged at actuals and explained upfront, so there are no surprises on the road. Our vehicles are commercially insured, our drivers are verified and know their routes well, and we operate 24/7, including all festivals. We don't make claims we can't back up — no fake review counts, no unverifiable awards — just a fixed fare, a known driver, and a car that shows up on time.

Who We Serve

Families and senior citizens travelling for darshan or sightseeing get comfortable AC cars, unhurried temple visits, and a driver who helps with boarding and luggage. Pilgrims heading to Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana, Kaila Devi, or Mehandipur Balaji get drivers who know the temple timings, parking, and local customs. First-time tourists get fixed fares confirmed in writing on WhatsApp, with no meter games and no unwanted shopping stops. Business and transfer travellers get on-time airport and railway station pickups, with buffer built in for flight or train delays.
