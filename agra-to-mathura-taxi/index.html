What People Usually See In Mathura

A private cab lets you set the order and the pace. These are the stops most customers ask for — pick the ones you want and tell us when you book.

Krishna Janmabhoomi

The birthplace of Lord Krishna and the main reason most visitors come. Your driver drops you at the entrance and waits nearby while you complete darshan.

Dwarkadhish Temple

One of Mathura’s best-known temples, in the old town near the ghats. Busiest around Janmashtami and Holi.

Vishram Ghat

The ghat on the Yamuna where, by tradition, Krishna rested after defeating Kansa. Best visited in the evening, for the aarti.

Mathura Museum

Kushan and Gupta period sculpture, worth an hour if you want the region’s history alongside its temples.

Kusum Sarovar

A historic sandstone reservoir near Govardhan, quiet compared with the town temples and popular for photographs.

Govardhan Hill

The sacred hill associated with Krishna, often added as a stop on the way back. Ask us before booking — it lengthens the day.

Agra To Mathura Taxi — Questions & Answers

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Mathura?

The Agra to Mathura taxi fare starts at ₹1,500 for a one-way trip in an AC sedan and ₹3,000 for a round trip. The fare covers fuel and driver charges. Tolls and parking are paid separately.

How far is Mathura from Agra by road?

Mathura is approximately 58 to 62 km from Agra by road via NH-2. The journey takes about 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes depending on traffic conditions.

Can I book a one-way cab from Agra to Mathura?

Yes. Both one-way and round-trip bookings are available. The one-way fare starts at ₹1,500 for an AC sedan. Call +91-87200-81102 or message us on WhatsApp to book.

What cars are available for an Agra to Mathura taxi?

AC sedans (Maruti Dzire, Honda Amaze) seat up to 4 passengers, AC SUVs (Maruti Ertiga, Toyota Innova) seat 6 to 7, and a Tempo Traveller is available for groups of 12 to 15.

Is the taxi available for early morning or late night?

Yes. The Agra to Mathura cab service runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including early-morning temple visits, late-night returns and connections to a train or flight.

Can the driver wait while I visit temples in Mathura?

Yes. On a round-trip booking your driver waits at each stop while you complete darshan, with no separate waiting charge. Take your time at Krishna Janmabhoomi, Dwarkadhish Temple and the other sites you want to see.

Can we add Vrindavan or Govardhan to the same trip?

Yes. Vrindavan is about 15 km beyond Mathura. We publish a full-day Mathura and Vrindavan tour from Agra at ₹3,000 and an extended Mathura, Vrindavan and Barsana day at ₹3,500, both for an AC sedan. Govardhan is run as its own route. Tell us on WhatsApp what you want to cover and we will confirm which booking fits.

How do I book an Agra to Mathura taxi?

Call +91-87200-81102 or send a WhatsApp message with your name, pickup point in Agra, travel date and number of passengers. We confirm the fare in writing and share your driver’s name, mobile number and car number before pickup. No app and no advance registration is needed.
