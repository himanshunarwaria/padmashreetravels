Route and Travel Time

Mehandipur Balaji Temple is in Dausa district, Rajasthan, and sits just off the Agra–Jaipur highway. From Agra, your cab takes the highway past Fatehpur Sikri and the Bharatpur bypass, then exits near Mahwa for the short approach road to the temple town. The road is good for most of the journey, and the drive takes about 2.5 to 3 hours each way depending on traffic and your pickup point in Agra.

We pick up from any address in Agra — your hotel, home, Agra Cantt railway station, Agra Fort station, or Kheria Airport. On the way, your driver can stop at a clean highway dhaba for tea, breakfast, or a washroom break. Just tell him — extra stops cost nothing.

Places Covered

What You'll Visit

Shri Mehandipur Balaji Temple

One of India's most famous Hanuman temples, known across the country for its powerful darshan and continuous prayers. The temple is open daily, with Tuesdays, Saturdays, and Hanuman Jayanti drawing the largest crowds. Plan 2–4 hours here depending on the day and the queue.

Nearby shrines in the temple town

Devotees often also visit the smaller shrines within the Mehandipur temple complex and the surrounding lanes. Your driver waits at the parking for as long as you need within the booked day.

Optional return stop: Fatehpur Sikri

The Mughal ghost city with the towering Buland Darwaza lies right on your return route to Agra. If your darshan finishes early, a 1–1.5 hour stop is easy to add — tell your driver in advance so parking is planned.

A respectful note for first-time visitors: Mehandipur Balaji has its own long-standing traditions — most famously, devotees do not carry prasad back home from this temple and traditionally do not look back at the temple when leaving. Our drivers make this route regularly and will politely guide first-time visitors on the customs, parking, and where to leave footwear.
