Booking — Questions & Answers

Do I have to pay anything to book?

No. There is no advance payment for most bookings — you pay after the ride. The form here does not take a card; it composes your trip details and hands them to us on WhatsApp, where we confirm the fare in writing.

What is your cancellation policy?

Ask us about cancellation before confirming your booking, and we will tell you where you stand. No advance payment is needed for most bookings, so in practice there is usually nothing to reclaim.

Is the fare shown on this page final?

It is an estimate for the route you picked, and it is what we expect to charge. The figure becomes fixed when we confirm it in writing on WhatsApp. Tolls and parking are extra and paid at actuals, and we tell you the expected toll before you agree.

What if there are five or more of us?

Choose 5–6 passengers and the form will tell you SUV pricing applies; the exact fare depends on the route and is confirmed on WhatsApp. For 7 or more, please call us — a Tempo Traveller is arranged per trip, so availability has to be checked rather than assumed.

Can I book for a date months ahead?

Yes, and you can also book for today — same-day bookings are usually possible and confirmed within minutes. For an early-morning departure or a larger vehicle, a day’s notice makes it certain.

I would rather not use a form. Can I just call?

Yes. Call +91-87200 81102 at any hour, or message the same number on WhatsApp. The form exists to save you typing out your trip details, not to replace talking to us.
