Agra To Shikohabad — Questions & Answers

How far is Shikohabad from Agra?

About 75 km on NH-19, roughly 1.5 hours each way. The round trip usually comes to 150 to 200 km once local movement is counted — 150 of that is simply the highway in both directions.

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Shikohabad?

₹3,000 for a round trip in an AC sedan, covering fuel and driver charges. Tolls and parking are paid by you at actuals. Ertiga, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller fares are quoted on WhatsApp, as is the one-way fare — we do not publish a one-way figure for this route.

Which district is Shikohabad in?

Shikohabad is a town in Firozabad district, Uttar Pradesh, east of Agra on NH-19. It sits between Firozabad and Etawah on the same road.

Can I do it as a day trip and come back the same evening?

Yes, and that is how nearly everyone books it. At about 1.5 hours each way there is plenty of room in a day to get there, deal with whatever you came for and be back in Agra by evening. One customer described doing exactly that.

Does the driver wait while I finish?

Yes. On a round trip the driver stays with you and waits while you do what you came to do, then takes you back. Tell us roughly how long you expect to need when you book, so the day is planned rather than guessed.

What is there to see in Shikohabad?

Honestly, this is not a sightseeing route, and we would rather say so than invent attractions. People book this cab to get something done — family matters, paperwork, a visit. If you want a day out, our Bateshwar temple trip or Gwalior fort day are better uses of the same money.

Can I book at short notice?

Usually, yes. Same-day bookings are normally possible and we confirm within minutes on WhatsApp. No advance payment is needed for most bookings, and we run 24 hours a day including holidays and festivals.

Can I book one way only?

Yes. We do not publish a one-way figure for this route, so ask on WhatsApp and we will quote it. If you want to be dropped in Shikohabad and collected on another day, tell us both dates and we will quote the whole thing together.
