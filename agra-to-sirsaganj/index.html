Agra To Sirsaganj — Questions & Answers

How far is Sirsaganj from Agra?

Between 80 and 90 km depending on your pickup point, usually about 85, and roughly two hours each way. The route runs through Firozabad on NH-19.

Is ₹3,000 the one-way or round-trip fare?

Round trip. It covers going to Sirsaganj, the driver waiting while you finish, and the drive back, in an AC sedan. Fuel and driver charges are included; tolls and parking are paid by you at actuals. We do not publish a one-way figure for this route — ask on WhatsApp and we will quote it.

Which district is Sirsaganj in?

Firozabad district, Uttar Pradesh, east of Agra. You pass through Firozabad itself on the way, and Shikohabad sits on the same road about 10 km short of Sirsaganj.

Can I do it as a day trip and return the same evening?

Yes, and that is how nearly everyone books it. At about two hours each way there is room in a day to get there, deal with what you came for and be back in Agra by evening.

Does the driver wait while I finish?

Yes. On a round trip the driver stays with you and waits, then takes you back when you are ready. Tell us roughly how long you expect to need when you book, so the day is planned rather than guessed.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel free of charge at least 12 hours before your scheduled departure. No advance payment is needed for most bookings, so in practice there is usually nothing to reclaim. If you need to cancel closer to departure, tell us as early as you can and we will confirm where you stand.

What is there to see in Sirsaganj?

This is not a sightseeing route, and we would rather say so than invent attractions. People book this cab to get something done. If you want a day out for similar money, our Bateshwar temple trip or Gwalior fort day are better uses of it.

Can I book at short notice?

Usually, yes. Same-day bookings are normally possible and we confirm within minutes on WhatsApp. Tell us your pickup time — including early-morning or late-night — and we confirm availability in writing before you book.
