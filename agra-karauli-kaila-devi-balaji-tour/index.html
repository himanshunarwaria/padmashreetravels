The Route, Planned Properly

The circuit runs: Agra → Mahwa → Mehandipur Balaji (morning darshan) → Karauli → Kaila Devi (afternoon darshan) → Hindaun → Agra. Balaji first works best on most days: you reach before the heaviest crowd, and the afternoon light suits the Kaila Devi leg. On certain days and during Navratri we may advise the reverse order — our drivers run this exact circuit regularly and we'll recommend the best sequence for your date when you message us.

An honest note before you book: This is a long but very rewarding day — around 12–13 hours with roughly 7 hours of driving. It suits groups that are comfortable with an early start. For groups with elderly members we recommend the Innova Crysta and, if your schedule allows, the relaxed 2-day version with a night halt in Karauli. Ask us for the 2-day fare — many families find it worth the small extra cost.

Places Covered

What You'll Visit

Shri Mehandipur Balaji Temple

The renowned Hanuman temple of Dausa district, visited by devotees from across India. Tuesdays and Saturdays are the most auspicious and the most crowded. Our drivers know the parking and will politely guide first-time visitors on the temple's customs — including the tradition of not carrying prasad back from this temple.

Kaila Devi Temple

The great Shakti temple on the Kalisil river near Karauli, at its most vibrant during the Chaitra Navratri fair. Expect a short walking stretch from the parking; your driver drops you at the closest permitted point.

Madan Mohan Ji Temple, Karauli (optional)

Karauli's historic Krishna temple — a serene 30-minute stop that many families call the sweetest surprise of the day.
