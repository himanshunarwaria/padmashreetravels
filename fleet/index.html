Fleet — Questions & Answers

Which cab is best for a family of five?

For a family of five with luggage, a Maruti Ertiga or Toyota Innova is the most comfortable choice. A sedan seats four passengers comfortably, so five travellers with bags are better in an SUV.

Are all your cabs air-conditioned?

Yes. All vehicles are air-conditioned and cleaned before every trip. We offer AC sedans, Ertiga and Innova SUVs, and a Tempo Traveller for groups.

Do you have a vehicle for a large group?

Yes, a Tempo Traveller is available for group pilgrimage and extended-family trips. Because larger vehicles are arranged per trip, please message us on WhatsApp with your group size and dates to confirm availability and the fare.

How do I choose and book a vehicle?

Tell us your route, number of passengers, and luggage on WhatsApp. We suggest the right vehicle and confirm the fixed fare in writing. Your driver and car details are shared before pickup.

Will I get exactly the car in the photograph?

Not necessarily, and we would rather say so. The photographs show the vehicle category. Exact make, trim and colour vary by availability, and some vehicles are arranged through trusted local operators rather than provided directly. What you are promised is the category you booked, air-conditioned and cleaned — and your actual car number and driver details are always confirmed before pickup.

How much luggage will actually fit?

The figures on this page are typical for each category, but they depend on the exact vehicle and how many people are travelling. Four passengers with four large suitcases will not fit a sedan comfortably even though it seats four. Tell us both numbers — passengers and bags — and we will suggest a size up if it would otherwise be a squeeze.

Where do I find the fare for each vehicle?

Fares depend on the route rather than the vehicle alone, so they are listed per route on the Agra taxi fare list rather than here. Send us your route and passenger count on WhatsApp and we will confirm a fixed fare in writing before you travel.
