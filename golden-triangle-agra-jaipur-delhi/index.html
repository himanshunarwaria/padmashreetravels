What This Page Is — And Is Not

"Golden Triangle" usually means Delhi, Agra and Jaipur seen as one trip. We are a private taxi operator based in Agra, not a Delhi or Jaipur agency, so this page does one honest job: it explains which parts of that circuit we can genuinely help with, gives you real distances and drive times for planning, and tells you plainly what is outside what we do. It does not sell a bundled multi-day package, because we do not have a fixed one to sell.

The Three Legs Of The Circuit

Delhi ↔ Agra

Approx. 230 km, 5–6 hours by road (our own figures are quoted for the Agra–Delhi Airport / IGI drop). We run this leg from the Agra side. See Agra to Delhi Airport Taxi.

Agra ↔ Jaipur

Approx. 235–240 km, 4–5 hours via NH-21, with Fatehpur Sikri and Bharatpur on the way. See Agra to Jaipur Taxi or, travelling the other direction, Jaipur to Agra Taxi.

Delhi ↔ Jaipur (direct)

This leg does not pass through Agra and is not a route we publish or operate. If your circuit needs it without an Agra stop, it would need a Delhi- or Jaipur-based operator.

The Usual Order — And Why It Varies

Most travellers fly into Delhi, drive to Agra for the Taj Mahal and other monuments, then continue on to Jaipur before flying home — or do the same circuit in reverse. Which direction suits you depends on your own flight bookings and where you can find international connections, not on anything we can decide for you. We are not inventing a "correct" itinerary here — just naming the pattern most people already follow, so you can plan around it.

Golden Triangle Planning — Questions & Answers

Does Padma Shree Travels run the whole Delhi-Agra-Jaipur Golden Triangle circuit?

We are an Agra-based operator and can genuinely help with the Agra to Delhi and Agra to Jaipur legs, in either direction, plus Agra sightseeing in between. We do not offer a fixed all-inclusive Golden Triangle package — every multi-day plan is discussed and quoted individually on WhatsApp.

How far is Agra from Delhi and from Jaipur?

Agra to Delhi (IGI Airport) is approximately 230 km, about 5–6 hours by road. Agra to Jaipur is approximately 235–240 km, about 4–5 hours by road via NH-21.

Can you arrange the Delhi to Jaipur leg directly, without going through Agra?

No. That direct leg does not pass through Agra and is not a route we publish or operate. If your circuit needs a direct Delhi–Jaipur transfer, that would need to be arranged separately from a Delhi- or Jaipur-based operator.

What is the usual order for a Golden Triangle trip?

Most travellers fly into Delhi, drive to Agra for the Taj Mahal and other monuments, then continue to Jaipur before flying out — or the reverse. Both directions are common; which one suits you depends on your flight bookings, not on anything we can decide for you.

Is there a fixed Golden Triangle package price?

No. We do not sell a bundled multi-day package with a set price. Each leg is quoted on its own fare list, and a multi-day plan combining legs is quoted individually on WhatsApp once we know your dates, stops and vehicle preference.

Can the same car and driver stay with us for the whole circuit?

For the legs we operate (Agra-Delhi and Agra-Jaipur), yes — tell us your full plan on WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly which parts we can cover with one car and driver, and which parts you would need to arrange separately.
