Fare Questions, Answered

Are Padma Shree Travels fares fixed or metered?

All fares are fixed and confirmed in writing on WhatsApp before your trip. There is no meter and no surge pricing. The price we quote is the price you pay, apart from tolls and parking which are paid separately.

What is included in the Agra taxi fare?

The fixed fare includes fuel, driver charges and an air-conditioned car. For sightseeing and temple trips, the driver waiting during your visit is included for the booked hours.

Are tolls and parking included in the fare?

No. Toll taxes and monument or temple parking fees are paid separately by the customer. We tell you the expected extras upfront on WhatsApp before you confirm, so there are no hidden surprises.

Do you charge night or extra waiting charges?

Waiting during darshan or monument visits within your booked hours is included. For trips that run longer than the booked hours, a small per-hour extension applies. Any night or extra-waiting charge is told to you in writing on WhatsApp before you confirm.

How do I get the exact fare for my route?

Send your route, travel date, pickup point and number of passengers on WhatsApp to +91-87200-81102, or call us. We reply with the exact fixed fare, the vehicle and the expected tolls or parking for your trip.

Is any advance payment required?

No advance payment is required for most bookings. You pay after the booking is confirmed or after the ride, as agreed. Your driver’s name and vehicle number are shared before pickup.
