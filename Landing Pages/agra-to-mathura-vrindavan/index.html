Questions & Answers

How much is the Mathura Vrindavan tour from Agra?

₹3,000 round trip by AC sedan for the full day, including fuel, driver and stops at all the major temples in both cities. Ertiga ₹3,800, Innova ₹4,500. Tolls and parking are paid separately.

Can Mathura and Vrindavan be covered in one day?

Yes. Vrindavan is only about 15 km from Mathura, so a 6–7 AM start from Agra covers the major temples of both comfortably in one 8–10 hour day.

What is the total distance?

Approximately 200–250 km round trip from Agra, with about 4–5 hours of actual driving time plus temple visit time.

How long does the tour take?

About 8–10 hours as a full day. Agra to Mathura is roughly 1.5 hours, Mathura to Vrindavan about 25 minutes, and you are usually back in Agra by 6–7 PM.

Which temples are covered?

In Mathura: Krishna Janmabhoomi, Dwarkadhish Temple and Vishram Ghat. In Vrindavan: Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir, ISKCON, Nidhivan, Seva Kunj and the Yamuna ghats. Additional stops can be added.

What time should we leave Agra?

We recommend a 6:00–7:00 AM pickup. The drive to Mathura takes about 1.5 hours, so an early start gives you unhurried darshan and still returns you to Agra the same evening.

Are fuel and driver included?

Yes. The fare includes fuel and driver charges for the full day, with the same cab and driver staying with you from pickup to return.

Are tolls and parking extra?

Yes — tolls and temple parking are paid at actuals and are not part of the package fare. We share the expected amounts on WhatsApp before you confirm.

Is the tour suitable for elderly people and children?

Absolutely. A private AC taxi is the most comfortable option — you can take breaks whenever needed, and the driver helps navigate crowded temple areas.

Can Barsana be added?

Yes, if you start early. Mathura + Vrindavan + Barsana is ₹3,500 for a sedan, ₹4,500 Ertiga and ₹5,500 Innova. Plan for a 10–12 hour day to cover all three cities comfortably.

Can Govardhan be added?

Yes. Mathura + Vrindavan + Govardhan is ₹3,500 sedan, ₹4,500 Ertiga and ₹5,500 Innova. The complete four-city circuit is ₹4,500 sedan, ₹5,500 Ertiga and ₹7,000 Innova.

Can we book during Janmashtami or Holi?

Yes, we operate during all festivals. Book 2–3 days in advance during Janmashtami, Holi and Radha Ashtami as demand is very high. Festival season fares may be slightly higher.
