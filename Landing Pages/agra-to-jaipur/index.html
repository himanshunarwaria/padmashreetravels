Questions & Answers

How far is Jaipur from Agra?

Roughly 235–240 km via NH-21 (also signed NH-11), passing Fatehpur Sikri, Bharatpur, Mahwa and Dausa.

How long does Agra to Jaipur take?

About 4 to 5 hours by private taxi, depending on traffic, your pickup point in Agra and the breaks you take.

Is one-way taxi available?

Yes. A one-way drop to Jaipur suits travellers continuing their trip from there rather than returning to Agra.

Is round trip available?

Yes. A round trip suits a day visit or a short Jaipur stay, with the same cab and driver bringing you back to Agra.

How much does an Agra to Jaipur taxi cost?

It depends on the cab type and whether you book one-way or round trip. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and we share today’s fixed fare before you book.

Are tolls included?

No. The fare covers the vehicle, fuel and driver. Highway tolls on NH-21 are paid at actuals, and we tell you the expected total before you book.

Can you pick me up from Agra Cantt?

Yes. We pick up from any Agra address including Agra Cantt, Agra Fort station and Kheria Airport.

Can you pick me up from my hotel?

Yes. Hotel pickup anywhere in Agra is standard on this route — share the hotel name and your preferred time.

Can we stop during the journey?

Yes. On-route breaks are included. Fatehpur Sikri is an easy 1–1.5 hour stop about 40 km out; only a longer detour off the route may change the fare.

When will driver details be shared?

Before pickup you receive the driver’s name, mobile number and car registration number on WhatsApp.
