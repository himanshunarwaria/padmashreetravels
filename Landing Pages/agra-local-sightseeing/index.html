Why Explore Agra By Private Cab?

One Cab All Day

No negotiating with a new auto or taxi driver at every monument. The same car and driver stay with you from pickup to final drop.

Escape The Heat

Agra gets hot and the monuments are spread across the city. You return to an air-conditioned car between stops instead of waiting on the roadside.

Keep Your Luggage

Bags stay safely in the car while you explore — useful if you have arrived at Agra Cantt or the airport and are moving on the same day.

Flexible Timing

Stay longer at the places you enjoy and skip the ones you don’t. Nothing on the itinerary is compulsory.

Local Route Knowledge

Our drivers know the practical routes between monuments, where the ticket counters are and which parking points actually work.

Easy Final Drop

End the day at your hotel, Agra Cantt railway station or Agra Airport — whichever suits your onward plans.

Here’s What Your Agra Sightseeing Day Can Look Like:

01 — Sunrise At The Taj Mahal

Start early for softer light, cooler weather and fewer crowds. Allow roughly 2–2.5 hours for a relaxed visit.

02 — Agra Fort

Continue to Agra Fort and explore its palaces, courtyards and famous views back toward the Taj Mahal.

03 — Baby Taj

Visit the Tomb of Itimad-ud-Daulah, known for its intricate marble inlay work and peaceful riverside gardens.

04 — Lunch Break

Pause for lunch whenever it fits your schedule. Your driver can help you reach suitable restaurants nearby.

05 — Akbar’s Tomb, Sikandra

Continue toward Sikandra to visit Emperor Akbar’s impressive mausoleum and its surrounding gardens.

06 — Mehtab Bagh

Finish the sightseeing portion with a beautiful view of the Taj Mahal from across the Yamuna.

This itinerary is a suggestion, not a fixed route. Skip monuments, spend longer at the Taj Mahal, start later, add Sadar Bazaar for marble handicrafts, leather goods and Agra’s famous petha, ask about adding Fatehpur Sikri, or finish with a railway station or airport drop. Tell us what you want and we’ll shape the day around it.

Questions & Answers

How much does Agra sightseeing cost?

Full day is ₹2,500 for an AC sedan and ₹3,500 for an AC SUV (8 hrs / 80 km). Half day is ₹1,500 sedan and ₹2,200 SUV (4 hrs / 40 km).

Which places are included?

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Baby Taj, Akbar’s Tomb at Sikandra, Mehtab Bagh and Sadar Bazaar — stops vary with the itinerary you choose.

Is monument entry included?

No. Entry tickets are separate and paid at each site. Tolls and parking are also paid by you at actuals.

Are fuel and driver charges included?

Yes. Every package fare includes the AC car, fuel and driver. Only tickets, tolls and parking are extra — told to you upfront.

Can I customise the tour?

Yes. Sightseeing is adjusted around your schedule and priorities — skip stops, add stops or start later.

Can you pick me up from Agra Cantt?

Yes, we pick up from any address in Agra including Agra Cantt, Agra Fort station, Kheria Airport and hotels.

Can I keep luggage in the car?

Yes. Your bags stay in the car through the day, which helps if you are arriving or leaving Agra the same day.

Can I add Fatehpur Sikri?

Yes. Full Day + Fatehpur Sikri is ₹3,500 for an AC sedan and ₹4,500 for an AC SUV.

Can you drop me at the airport or station afterwards?

Yes. Airport Pickup + Sightseeing is ₹2,800 sedan / ₹3,800 SUV. A transfer on its own starts at ₹800.

What time should I begin?

A 5:30–6:00 AM start gives you the Taj Mahal cooler and less crowded, usually finishing by 4–5 PM. A later start is fine too.
