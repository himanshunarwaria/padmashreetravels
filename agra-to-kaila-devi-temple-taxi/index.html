The Same-Day Plan

Times shift with the queue, and considerably during Navratri. This is the shape of the one-day booking.

  • 5:00 am — pickup from your Agra hotel or home.
  • 7:00 am — tea and breakfast break near Hindaun.
  • 9:00 am — arrive; the driver drops you at the closest permitted point and parks.
  • 9:00–11:30 am — darshan, and time at the Kalisil ghat if you want it.
  • 12:00 noon — optional stop at Madan Mohan Ji Temple in Karauli town, 30–45 minutes.
  • 1:00 pm — lunch in Karauli or on the highway.
  • 7:00–8:00 pm — drop back at your Agra address.
  • Prefer an easier pace? Ask for the two-day plan with a night halt in Karauli — a common choice for families travelling with elderly parents.

What The Day Covers

Kaila Devi Mata Mandir

A major Shakti temple of eastern Rajasthan, and the destination of the annual Kaila Devi Fair, which draws lakhs of devotees during Chaitra Navratri. Allow 2 to 3 hours on a normal day — considerably more during Navratri.

Kalisil River Ghat

The ghat beside the temple is a quiet spot for a few minutes after darshan, and pleasant in the cooler months.

Madan Mohan Ji Temple, Karauli — optional

Karauli town’s Krishna temple, closely connected with the Braj tradition. A 30 to 45 minute stop on the way back. Karauli’s old City Palace is nearby if you want a short heritage detour — tell your driver.

Navratri — what to expect

During the Kaila Devi Fair (Chaitra Navratri, usually March–April) and again during Sharad Navratri, roads near the temple are diverted and extremely busy, and there is a longer walking stretch from the parking to the temple. Book your cab several days in advance for these dates, start early, and carry water. Our drivers know the diversions and the correct parking on fair days.

Agra To Kaila Devi — Questions & Answers

How far is Kaila Devi Temple from Agra?

Kaila Devi Temple is approximately 165 to 175 km from Agra, about 3.5 to 4 hours by private taxi via Fatehpur Sikri, the Bharatpur bypass and Hindaun. The temple itself is around 23 km beyond Karauli town, on the banks of the Kalisil river.

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Kaila Devi?

We do not publish a fixed starting price for this route. A same-day round trip is available in an AC sedan, with Ertiga, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller for families and groups, and a two-day fare with a night halt in Karauli on request. Tolls, Rajasthan state tax where applicable, and parking are extra at actuals and told to you before booking. Message us on WhatsApp for today’s fixed fare.

Can we do Agra to Kaila Devi darshan in one day?

Yes. With a 5:00 am pickup you reach the temple by around 9:00 am, get unhurried darshan plus an optional stop in Karauli town, and are back in Agra by 7:00 to 8:00 pm. Families travelling with elderly members sometimes prefer the relaxed two-day version instead.

What does the two-day trip with a night halt include?

The two-day version gives you a night halt in Karauli, so the drive is split and darshan is unhurried. The driver’s night allowance is already included in the fare we quote, so it is not added afterwards. Your own accommodation and meals are separate, and we can suggest where to stay in Karauli if that helps.

When is the Kaila Devi Fair, and should we book earlier for it?

The Kaila Devi Fair falls during Chaitra Navratri, usually in March or April, and the temple is also very busy during Sharad Navratri in September or October. For those dates, book your cab at least 3 to 5 days in advance, choose the earliest pickup you can, and expect a longer walk from the parking because roads near the temple are diverted.

Can we combine Kaila Devi with Mehandipur Balaji in one day?

Yes. The two temples combine into a full-day Rajasthan circuit of roughly 350 to 380 km, about 12 to 13 hours door to door. See our Karauli, Kaila Devi and Mehandipur Balaji tour page for the planned itinerary and fare.

Is the trip comfortable for senior citizens?

Yes, with the right planning. We suggest the Innova Crysta for the longer drive, schedule breaks near Hindaun both ways, and the driver drops you at the closest permitted point to the temple. There is still a walking stretch from there, and it is longer during Navratri. Tell us on WhatsApp about any special needs — extra stops cost nothing.

Is it Kaila Devi, Kailadevi or Kela Devi? And Karauli or Karoli?

All of these refer to the same place. Whether you search Kaila Devi, Kailadevi or Kela Devi Mata Mandir, it is the same Shakti temple near Karauli — a town whose name is also commonly spelled Karoli. We run cabs there from Agra throughout the year.
