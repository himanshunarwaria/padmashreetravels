Route and Travel Time

Kaila Devi Temple stands on the banks of the Kalisil river, about 23 km from Karauli town in eastern Rajasthan. From Agra, your cab takes the highway towards Fatehpur Sikri and the Bharatpur bypass, continues via Hindaun, and then follows the Karauli road to the temple. Expect about 3.5 to 4 hours of driving each way. Most of the route is decent highway; the final stretch is a smaller temple-town road where an experienced driver makes a real difference.

We pick up from any Agra address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt, Agra Fort station, or Kheria Airport — and plan a tea/washroom break near Hindaun on the way.

Places Covered

What You'll Visit

Kaila Devi Temple (Kaila Devi Mata Mandir)

One of the most revered Shakti temples of the region, drawing lakhs of devotees — especially during the annual Kaila Devi Fair in Chaitra Navratri. Plan 2–3 hours here on a normal day; considerably more during Navratri.

Kalisil River Ghat

The ghat beside the temple is a peaceful spot for a few minutes after darshan, especially in the cooler months.

Madan Mohan Ji Temple, Karauli (optional)

Karauli town's beloved Krishna temple, closely connected with the Braj tradition, makes a beautiful 30–45 minute stop on your way back. Karauli's old City Palace is nearby if you'd like a short heritage detour — just tell your driver.

Navratri note: During the Kaila Devi Fair (Chaitra Navratri, usually March–April) and Sharad Navratri, roads near the temple are diverted and extremely busy, and you should expect a longer walking stretch from the parking to the temple. Book your cab several days in advance for these dates, start early, and carry water. Our drivers know the diversions and the correct parking for fair days.
