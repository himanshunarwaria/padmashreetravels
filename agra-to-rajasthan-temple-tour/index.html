One Day, Two Days, Or More?

This is the decision that shapes the whole trip. Work out which row you are in and the rest follows.

One Day

Mehandipur Balaji or Kaila Devi, on its own. Either makes a comfortable single-day trip with an early-morning pickup and an evening return to Agra. Karauli town fits here too, with Kaila Devi as an add-on.

Two Days

Both temples, without the endurance test. The two-temple circuit can be done in one day — about 12 to 13 hours door to door, roughly eight of them driving — but the two-day version with a night halt in Karauli spreads it out. This is what we recommend when elderly parents are travelling.

Three Days Or More

Khatu Shyam Ji and Salasar Balaji. Both lie further into Rajasthan, so the distance alone makes them a multi-day yatra. They are planned entirely to your dates, and are often combined with each other.

When These Temples Are Busiest

Kaila Devi

  • Chaitra Navratri, usually March–April, is the Kaila Devi Fair and the busiest time of the year.
  • Sharad Navratri, September–October, is the second peak.
  • On both, roads near the temple are diverted and there is a longer walk from the parking. Book several days ahead and start early.

Mehandipur Balaji

  • Tuesdays and Saturdays are considered the most auspicious and draw the largest crowds.
  • Hanuman Jayanti is the busiest single day.
  • For any of these, book 2–3 days ahead and take the earliest pickup you can manage.

Why A Private Cab For This Yatra

  • A shared bus tour runs on a fixed group schedule, regardless of how long your darshan takes.
  • With a private cab, the pickup time, the pace and the route are yours.
  • Our drivers run these Rajasthan routes regularly and know the parking closest to each temple.
  • The car waits as long as you need within the booked day.
  • Being honest about it: for someone travelling alone, a bus will usually cost less per head. A private cab pays off with a family, or when darshan time is unpredictable.

Rajasthan Temple Tours — Questions & Answers

Which Rajasthan temples can we visit from Agra?

The most popular are Mehandipur Balaji in Dausa district and Kaila Devi Temple near Karauli, which can be visited separately or combined into a single-day or two-day circuit. Karauli town itself, with the Madan Mohan Ji Temple and City Palace, is on the same road. Khatu Shyam Ji and Salasar Balaji are further into Rajasthan and are planned as custom multi-day tours.

Should we do a one-day, two-day, or multi-day tour?

Mehandipur Balaji or Kaila Devi alone work well as one-day trips. Combining both in one day is possible but long — about 12 to 13 hours door to door, roughly eight of them in the car. A two-day version with a night halt in Karauli spreads the same circuit into a comfortable pace, and it is what we recommend for families travelling with elderly members. Khatu Shyam Ji and Salasar Balaji, being further away, need three days or more.

How much does a Rajasthan temple tour from Agra cost?

It depends on the temples, the distance, the number of days and the cab. Message us on WhatsApp with the temples you want to cover and the days you have, and we will quote one fixed fare. We do not invent a price without knowing your exact plan, which is why there is no number on this page.

Is a private cab better than a group bus tour for this yatra?

For most pilgrim families, yes. A private cab means your own pickup time, your own pace at each temple, and a driver who waits as long as your darshan takes. A bus tour runs on a fixed group schedule that cannot offer that, though it will usually cost less per person for someone travelling alone.

Is this suitable for senior citizens and families?

Yes, with the right planning. We suggest the Innova Crysta for longer Rajasthan routes, plan tea and washroom breaks around your comfort, and the driver assists with boarding and luggage at every stop. For longer yatras we usually recommend the relaxed multi-day version rather than a single very long day, and there is a stair climb at Kaila Devi worth knowing about in advance.

When are these temples busiest?

Kaila Devi is busiest during Chaitra Navratri, which is the Kaila Devi Fair, and again during Sharad Navratri, when roads near the temple are diverted and there is a longer walk from the parking. Mehandipur Balaji is busiest on Tuesdays, Saturdays and Hanuman Jayanti. For any of these dates, book several days ahead and take the earliest pickup you can.

Can you plan a custom itinerary covering multiple temples?

Yes. Tell us the temples you want to cover — Kaila Devi, Mehandipur Balaji, Karauli, Khatu Shyam Ji, Salasar Balaji or others — along with your available days, and we will plan a realistic route and quote one fixed fare. If your list will not fit the days you have, we say so before you book.
