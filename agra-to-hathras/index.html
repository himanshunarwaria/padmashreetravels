Agra To Hathras — Questions & Answers

How far is Hathras from Agra?

Approximately 55 to 60 km, about an hour and a half each way. It is short enough to be a half-day trip rather than a full one.

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Hathras?

₹2,000 for a round trip in an AC sedan — going, waiting and coming back. Fuel and driver charges are included; tolls and parking are paid by you at actuals. Ertiga, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller fares are quoted on WhatsApp, as is the one-way fare.

Can I book a cab for someone else?

Yes. Send us the passenger’s name and number with the trip details and we deal with them directly from there — they receive the driver’s name, number and car registration exactly as you would. The fare is still confirmed in writing to you, so whoever is paying knows the figure in advance.

Can you pick up early in the morning?

Yes. Tell us your pickup time — including early-morning or late-night — and we confirm availability in writing before you book. Your fare is confirmed in writing before travel, including for early-morning or late-night pickup requests.

Does the driver wait while I finish?

Yes. On a round trip the driver stays with you and waits, then takes you back when you are ready rather than at a fixed time. Tell us roughly how long you expect to need when you book.

Can I book one way only?

Yes. We do not publish a one-way figure for this route, so ask on WhatsApp and we will quote it. If you need a drop one day and a pickup on another, tell us both dates and we will quote them together.

Can I book at short notice?

Usually, yes. Same-day bookings are normally possible and we confirm within minutes on WhatsApp. No advance payment is needed for most bookings.

Where in Agra will you pick me up?

Any address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt, Agra Fort station or Kheria Airport — and you are dropped back at the same place at the end.
