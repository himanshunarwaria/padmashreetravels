Route and Travel Time

The drive from Agra to Delhi's IGI Airport covers the Yamuna Expressway and NH44, roughly 230 km. Under normal conditions this takes about 3.5 to 4 hours, but Delhi traffic near the airport can add time unpredictably, especially during peak hours. We plan your pickup time with this in mind.

Flight Buffer Advice

We recommend leaving Agra at least 5.5 to 6 hours before an international flight and 4.5 to 5 hours before a domestic flight, to allow for the drive, possible traffic on the expressway and Delhi roads, and time for check-in and security at the airport. Please share your exact flight time and terminal when booking so we can suggest a safe pickup time — we plan generously for traffic, but we cannot guarantee an exact arrival time in unpredictable conditions, so building in extra buffer is always the safer choice.
