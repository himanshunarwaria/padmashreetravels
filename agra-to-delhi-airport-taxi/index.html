Agra To Delhi Airport Taxi — Questions & Answers

How far is Delhi Airport from Agra?

Delhi’s Indira Gandhi International (IGI) Airport is approximately 230 km from Agra via the Yamuna Expressway and NH44 — about 3.5 to 4 hours by private taxi, traffic permitting.

What is the taxi fare from Agra to Delhi Airport?

The fare depends on the cab type and exact pickup point in Agra. Message us on WhatsApp with your flight time and terminal for a fixed quote — tolls are extra at actuals and told to you before booking.

How much buffer time should we leave for the drop?

We recommend leaving Agra at least 5.5 to 6 hours before an international flight and 4.5 to 5 hours before a domestic flight, to allow for the drive, traffic on the Yamuna Expressway and Delhi roads, and airport security lines. We cannot guarantee an exact arrival time in unpredictable traffic, so extra buffer is always safer.

Which terminal will we be dropped at?

IGI Airport has three terminals — T1, T2 and T3. Share your terminal number along with your flight details when booking so your driver takes you to the correct one.

Can you pick us up from a hotel, home, or Agra Cantt?

Yes. We pick up from any Agra address — hotel, home, Agra Cantt railway station, or Kheria Airport — whichever is convenient for you.

Is this a one-way trip or can we book a round trip?

Both are available. Most customers book a one-way drop to Delhi Airport, but a return pickup can also be arranged if you are travelling back to Agra later.
