How The Booking Handoff Works

01 — Guest Is Pointed To Us

A desk mention, a printed card, or a QR code at reception — whichever suits your property.

02 — Guest Books Directly

By WhatsApp, phone, or our booking page. We ask every guest how they heard about us, and a QR/link source tells us automatically.

03 — Fare Confirmed, Driver Sent

Same process as any booking: fare in writing, then driver name, phone and car number before pickup.

04 — Your Property Is Not In The Loop Operationally

We handle the trip end to end. Your desk's job is the introduction, not managing the booking.

How We’d Know A Booking Came From You

A QR code or link made for your property carries standard tracking parameters (UTM) - for example a source and medium label such as "your-hotel-name" and "qr". Our booking page already reads and stores these when a visitor arrives. Honestly: today that capture does not yet flow all the way through to the final booking record automatically - we are aware of this and it is on our list to fix before any partner QR code is printed and put on a desk (see our internal tracking plan). Until then, we also ask every caller and WhatsApp enquiry how they heard about us, which is the reliable fallback.

Partner Programme — Questions & Answers

What does the partner referral involve?

A hotel or homestay tells its guests about Padma Shree Travels — by a desk mention, a printed card, or a QR code — and the guest books directly with us by WhatsApp, call, or our booking page. Your guest gets the same fixed-fare, driver-details-in-advance service as any other customer.

Is there a commission for the property?

This is not yet finalised. Any referral or commission arrangement would be agreed individually with each property and confirmed in writing before it applies — nothing is assumed or automatic.

Does the guest pay more because of the referral?

No. The guest is quoted the same fare as any other customer booking the same route, confirmed in writing on WhatsApp before travel.

How do you know a booking came from our property?

A QR code or link with your property's own tracking parameters (UTM) tells us the source. If a guest calls or messages directly and mentions your property by name, that works too — we ask on every booking.

What services can we refer guests for?

Airport and railway station transfers, Agra sightseeing, Taj Mahal visits, Mathura-Vrindavan and other temple trips, and outstation routes including Jaipur and Delhi — the same services listed on our main site.

How do we become a partner?

Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone to discuss your property and what would work for your guests. Terms are agreed individually, in writing, before anything goes live.
