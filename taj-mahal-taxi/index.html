Taj Mahal Gate, Parking & Access Notes

The Taj Mahal has three visitor gates — East, West, and South. We typically drop visitors at the East Gate, which is usually less crowded than the West Gate. Private vehicles cannot enter the roughly 500-metre no-vehicle zone around the monument, so your driver parks at the designated lot and a short walk or battery-operated cart covers the final stretch. Lockers are available near the gates for bags and electronics not permitted inside.

The Taj Mahal is closed to sightseeing visitors every Friday (open only for those attending prayers at the mosque within the complex) — plan your visit on any other day of the week.
