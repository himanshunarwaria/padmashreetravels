Planning Your Taj Mahal Visit

Three gates, and the East Gate is usually easiest

The Taj Mahal has three visitor gates — East, West and South. We typically drop visitors at the East Gate, which is usually less crowded than the West Gate. Your driver will advise the best gate for the day and time of your visit.

The car cannot go right up to the monument

Private vehicles cannot enter the no-vehicle zone around the Taj Mahal. Your driver parks at the designated lot, and a short walk or a battery-operated cart covers the final stretch. Lockers are available near the gates for bags and electronics not permitted inside.

Closed every Friday

The Taj Mahal is closed to sightseeing visitors every Friday, open only to those attending prayers at the mosque inside the complex. Plan your visit on any other day of the week.

Choose your own time of day

Early morning, midday or a late-afternoon visit are all options — tell us your preference on WhatsApp and the driver plans the pickup around it. If sunrise itself is the goal, see our Taj Mahal sunrise taxi for that specific timing.

Taj Mahal Taxi — Questions & Answers

What is the taxi fare for a Taj Mahal visit?

A half-day taxi covering the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort starts at ₹1,500 for an AC sedan, about 4 hours and 40 km. If you would like to see all five major monuments in one day, the full-day sightseeing tour starts at ₹2,500.

Which gate should we use to enter the Taj Mahal?

The Taj Mahal has three entry gates — East, West and South. The East Gate is usually less crowded and is where we typically drop visitors; your driver will advise the best gate based on the day and time of your visit.

Can the taxi drop us right at the Taj Mahal entrance?

No. Vehicles cannot go right up to the monument because of a no-vehicle zone around the Taj Mahal. Your driver drops you as close as permitted, and a short walk or battery-operated cart covers the last stretch.

Is the Taj Mahal closed on any day of the week?

Yes. The Taj Mahal is closed to sightseeing visitors every Friday, open only to those attending prayers at the mosque inside the complex. Plan your visit on another day of the week.

Will the driver wait while we visit the Taj Mahal?

Yes. Your driver waits at the designated parking area for the duration of your visit, with no separate waiting charge within your booked time.

Can we combine the Taj Mahal with Agra Fort and other monuments?

Yes. The half-day package covers the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort together, and the full-day tour adds the Baby Taj, Akbar’s Tomb at Sikandra and Mehtab Bagh. Ask us on WhatsApp for the option that suits your schedule.
