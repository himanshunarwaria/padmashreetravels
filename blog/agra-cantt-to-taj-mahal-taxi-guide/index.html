Agra Cantt to Taj Mahal: Taxi Guide for Day-Trippers

Arriving by Gatimaan Express, Vande Bharat, or any morning train? Here's how to get from the platform to the Taj — and back for your evening train — without a single wasted minute.

Quick Answer — Agra Cantt to Taj Mahal

Distance: roughly 5–7 km, about 15–25 minutes by cab

Transfer fare: from ₹800 (AC sedan)

Thousands of travellers do Agra as a same-day train trip — morning train in, evening train out. It works beautifully if the ground transport is sorted before you arrive. Pickup from Agra Cantt

Book before you board: share your train number and scheduled arrival time on WhatsApp, and we take it from there. Your driver's name, phone number, and car registration number reach you before arrival, and the driver waits at the station exit when your train pulls in — if the train runs late, the pickup adjusts with it. No hunting for a fair price on the station forecourt after a long journey.

How Much Time to Keep for the Taj Mahal

Budget 2–3 hours for the Taj Mahal itself: the security queue, ticket check, and a proper unhurried walk through the complex. Cars can't enter the roughly 500-metre no-vehicle zone around the monument — your driver drops you at the designated parking, and the last stretch is a short walk or battery-cart ride. On weekends and holidays, queues run longer, so add buffer. And plan around Friday: the Taj Mahal is closed to sightseeing visitors on Fridays.

Luggage and Waiting

Your bags stay locked in the car at every stop, with the driver staying with the vehicle — convenient, and practically necessary, since large bags aren't allowed inside the Taj Mahal. Waiting at each monument is included within your booked day; there's no meter ticking while you take your time.

Which Cab to Book

An AC sedan handles couples and small families comfortably. Groups of five or more, or anyone with bulky luggage, should take the Ertiga or Innova Crysta. Flying in instead of taking the train? The same logic applies from Kheria Airport — see the Agra airport taxi page.

💡 Pro Tip

Book the return leg at the same time as the pickup. Evening trains from Agra Cantt fill the station road with traffic around departure time — a driver who already knows your train leaves nothing to chance.
