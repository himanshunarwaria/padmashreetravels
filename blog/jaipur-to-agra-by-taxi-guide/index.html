Jaipur to Agra by Taxi: Distance, Time & Same-Day Taj Trip

How long the drive takes, where to stop, and an honest answer on seeing the Taj Mahal and returning to Jaipur the same day.

Padma Shree TravelsNovember 20266 min read

Quick Answer — Jaipur to Agra by Taxi

Distance: about 235–240 km via NH-21

Drive time: 4–5 hours without long stops

Same-day Taj trip: possible, but leave Jaipur by 4–5 AM

Taj Mahal closed: every Friday

Fare: fixed fare confirmed on WhatsApp before you travel

Most people in Jaipur who search for this route want one thing: to see the Taj Mahal. The road is simple. The real decision is whether to do it as one long day or stay a night in Agra. We drive this highway from the Agra end every week, so here is the plain version.

How Far Is Agra from Jaipur?

Agra is about 235 to 240 km from Jaipur on NH-21. A straight drive takes 4 to 5 hours. The exact time depends on where in Jaipur you start and how early you leave. Getting out of Jaipur after 8:00 AM can easily add 30 minutes.

The Route

Jaipur → Dausa → Mahwa → Bharatpur → Fatehpur Sikri → Agra. It is a four-lane highway almost all the way, with dhabas and fuel stations at regular gaps. There is one state border, between Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, just after Bharatpur.

Same Day or Overnight?

A same-day return is possible. It is also a 9 to 10 hour day on the road, so it helps to see both options side by side.

If you can spare the night, take it. The Taj Mahal at sunrise is a different experience from the Taj at noon, and you arrive rested instead of straight off the highway. If you only have one day, it still works. Just start early and keep the plan to the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort.

💡 Check the day

The Taj Mahal is closed every Friday. If your only free day is a Friday, change the plan before you book the cab.

Stops Worth Making on the Way

Chand Baori, Abhaneri (roughly 90 km from Jaipur)

A very deep, very old stepwell a short detour off NH-21 near Sikandra. It takes 30 to 45 minutes and is the stop most travellers say they are glad they made.

Mehandipur Balaji (near Mahwa)

A well-known Hanuman temple close to the highway. Keep 2 to 3 hours if you want darshan. Our Mehandipur Balaji guide explains the customs.

Bharatpur (about 55 km before Agra)

Keoladeo National Park is beside the highway and is at its best from October to February. See our Bharatpur bird sanctuary guide.

Fatehpur Sikri (about 40 km before Agra)

Akbar's old capital is the last big stop before Agra. Give it 2 to 3 hours. It fits well on an overnight plan and is too much for a same-day return.

Pick one stop, or two short ones. All four in one drive turns a transfer into a very long day.
