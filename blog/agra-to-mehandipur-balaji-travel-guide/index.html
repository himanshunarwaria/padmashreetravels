Agra to Mehandipur Balaji: Complete Darshan Travel Guide

Distance, road, best days, temple customs, and a realistic same-day plan for visiting Shri Mehandipur Balaji Temple from Agra.

Padma Shree TravelsJuly 20269 min read

Quick Facts — Mehandipur Balaji

Location: Dausa district, Rajasthan

Distance from Agra: approx. 110–120 km

Drive time: 2.5–3 hours by taxi via the Agra–Jaipur highway

Busiest days: Tuesday and Saturday

Trip type: comfortable same-day round trip

Shri Mehandipur Balaji Temple is one of India's most visited Hanuman temples, drawing devotees from across the country for its powerful darshan and continuous prayers. For travellers based in Agra, it's a manageable day trip — but the temple's unique customs and the busy Tuesday/Saturday crowds are worth knowing about before you go.

How Far Is Mehandipur Balaji from Agra?

Mehandipur Balaji Temple sits in Dausa district, Rajasthan, just off the Agra–Jaipur highway, approximately 110–120 km from Agra. By private taxi, the drive takes about 2.5 to 3 hours each way, passing Fatehpur Sikri and the Bharatpur bypass before exiting near Mahwa for the short approach road to the temple town.

Best Days — and Which Ones to Avoid If You Want It Quiet

Tuesdays and Saturdays are considered the most auspicious for Balaji (Hanuman ji) and are, unsurprisingly, the busiest — along with Hanuman Jayanti. If your schedule allows, a Wednesday, Thursday, or Sunday visit with an early start will be noticeably calmer. Whatever day you choose, arriving by 8:00–8:30 AM gets you ahead of the main crowd.

Temple Customs Every First-Time Visitor Should Know

Mehandipur Balaji has its own long-standing traditions that differ from most temples. Most notably, devotees traditionally do not carry prasad back home from this temple, and many also avoid looking back at the temple when leaving. These customs are taken seriously by regular visitors, so it's worth knowing them in advance rather than being caught off guard. Local drivers who run this route regularly can point out where to leave footwear, where the queues form, and answer questions politely for first-timers.

💡 Pro Tip

Carry minimal loose items into the complex, follow the queue discipline at the gate, and ask your driver about parking before you get out — the temple town's lanes get congested on peak days.

Thinking of Adding Kaila Devi Temple?

Many pilgrim families combine Mehandipur Balaji with Kaila Devi Temple near Karauli into a single, longer darshan day. It's roughly 350–380 km in total and takes about 12–13 hours door to door — a long day, but a very doable one with an early start.
