Taxi Fare in Agra: What a Cab Costs & What's Included

Real prices for the common trips, what the fare covers, and the five questions to ask any taxi operator before you say yes.

Padma Shree TravelsNovember 20266 min read

Quick Answer — Taxi Fare in Agra

Airport or station transfer: from ₹800

Taj Mahal half day: from ₹1,500 (AC sedan)

Full-day Agra sightseeing: ₹2,500 for 8 hours / 80 km

Mathura round trip: ₹3,000

Included: AC car, fuel, driver

Extra: tolls, parking, entry tickets

"How much should a taxi cost in Agra?" is the question every visitor asks, and usually after someone has already quoted them a number. Taxis here do not run on meters. Fares are agreed before the trip. That is fine when the fare is clear, and a problem when it is not. Below are our own published fares, so you have a real reference point.

What the Fare Includes

  • An air-conditioned car for the booked hours or route
  • Fuel
  • The driver's charges
  • Waiting time while you visit a monument or temple, within the booked hours

What Is Extra

  • Tolls: on highways and expressways, paid at actuals.
  • Parking: at monuments and temples.
  • Entry tickets: for monuments and parks.
  • Extra hours: if a local trip runs past the booked hours or kilometres, a per-hour extension applies. Ask for that rate in writing before you book.

Sedan or SUV?

Five Questions to Ask Before You Book Any Taxi

  1. Is this the final fare, and can you send it in writing? A WhatsApp message is enough.
  2. Are tolls and parking included or extra? If extra, roughly how much?
  3. How many hours and kilometres does it cover? And what happens after that?
  4. Which car is it? "AC car" can mean many things. Ask for the model.
  5. Will the driver wait at each stop? Or is it only a drop?

Any honest operator answers all five without hesitation. If someone avoids the questions, that tells you what you need to know.

💡 About very cheap quotes

A quote far below everyone else usually leaves something out, or comes with long stops at shops that pay commission. Agree before you start that there will be no shopping stops unless you ask for one.
