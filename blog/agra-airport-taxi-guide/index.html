Agra Airport Taxi Guide: Pickup, Drop & Sightseeing Combos

Everything about landing at Kheria Airport — how the pickup works, what to share when booking, and the combos that turn a transfer into a full Agra day.

Padma Shree TravelsJuly 20268 min read

Quick Answer — Agra Airport Taxi

Airport: Kheria Airport / Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyay Airport (AGR)

City transfer: from ₹800, AC sedan

Pickup: name board at arrivals, driver details shared in advance

Combos: airport + Taj Mahal sightseeing, or onward to Mathura/Jaipur

To book: share flight number, date/time & drop location on WhatsApp

Agra's Kheria Airport is small, which is mostly good news: arrivals are quick, and your cab is a two-minute walk from the exit. The part worth planning is what happens next — a simple hotel drop, a straight-to-the-Taj sightseeing day, or an onward journey to Mathura or Jaipur. This guide covers all three.

How the Airport Pickup Works

Book before you fly. Your driver's name, phone number, and car registration number are shared with you on WhatsApp before your flight lands, and the driver waits at arrivals with a name board. Match the number plate, board, and go — no negotiating at the taxi rank after a flight.

What to Share When Booking

Three details make the pickup seamless: your flight number, your arrival date and time, and your drop location (hotel name or address). The flight number matters most — we track the schedule and adjust the pickup automatically if your flight is delayed, at no extra charge within a reasonable window.

Airport to Hotel Transfer

The simplest option: a direct drop at your Agra hotel or home, from ₹800 in an AC sedan. The exact fare depends on the drop location, confirmed as one fixed price on WhatsApp before you travel.

Airport + Taj Mahal Sightseeing Combo

Landing in the morning? Many travellers drop their luggage and head straight into a sightseeing day: the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and more, with the driver keeping your bags safely in the car between stops. Ask for the combined airport-pickup + full-day sightseeing (₹2,500) price when booking — it's usually better value than separate bookings. If you only want the headline monuments, the Taj Mahal taxi (from ₹1,500) covers the half-day version.

Onward to Mathura, Vrindavan or Jaipur

Kheria works well as a starting point for the wider region too. Onward journeys to Mathura–Vrindavan, a temple tour, or Jaipur can begin straight from the arrivals gate — tell us your plan and we'll quote one combined fare. Flying out of Delhi instead? We also run the Agra to Delhi Airport (IGI) drop with proper flight-time buffer planning.

💡 Pro Tip

For departures from Kheria, ask us to plan the pickup with a sensible buffer — the airport is small and quick, but traffic between the city and the airport varies by hour, and we'd rather you wait ten minutes at the gate than watch it from the road.

Flight Delay Buffer

Delays happen — that's why the flight number matters. We monitor arrivals and shift the pickup to match reality, not the original schedule. For departures, we recommend leaving earlier than feels necessary; exact road timing can't be guaranteed in unpredictable traffic, so buffer is your friend. Arriving by train instead? The same pickup logic applies at Agra Cantt — see our railway station taxi page.
