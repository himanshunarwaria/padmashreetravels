Golden Triangle by Road: Delhi, Agra & Jaipur Route Guide

Three cities, two long drives through Agra, and the question everyone asks first: how many days do we need?

Padma Shree TravelsNovember 20267 min read

Quick Answer — Golden Triangle by Road

Delhi to Agra: about 230 km, 3.5–4 hours via the Yamuna Expressway

Agra to Jaipur: about 240 km, 4–5 hours via NH-21

Jaipur to Delhi: roughly 270 km, about 5 hours

Days needed: 5–6 is comfortable; 4 is the minimum

What we cover: Agra sightseeing and the Agra–Jaipur and Agra–Delhi legs

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur sit roughly 230 to 270 km from each other, which makes a neat triangle on the map. It is the most popular first trip to India, and nearly all of it is done by road. We are an Agra operator, so we know two sides of this triangle very well. This guide tells you what the driving is like and is clear about which parts we can do for you.

How Many Days Do You Need?

7+Add Bharatpur, Mathura–Vrindavan or RanthamboreRelaxed, with something beyond the big three.

If you can, give Agra a night. That lets you see the Taj Mahal at sunrise, which is the best hour for it. Check your dates too: the Taj Mahal is closed every Friday.

Which Direction?

Delhi → Agra → Jaipur → Delhi is the usual order. The reverse works just as well. Let your flights decide. If you fly out of Jaipur, end there and skip the long drive back to Delhi.

Delhi to Agra

You have two good choices. The fast morning trains reach Agra Cantt in about two hours, and we can meet you at the station and start sightseeing straight away. Or you can come by car on the Yamuna Expressway. For the return to Delhi Airport at the end of a trip, see our guide on when to leave Agra for a Delhi flight.

Agra to Jaipur: The Best Drive of the Three

This leg has the most to see on the way, so do not treat it as a transfer.

  • Fatehpur Sikri (40 km from Agra): Akbar's abandoned capital. Allow 2 to 3 hours. See the Fatehpur Sikri guide.
  • Bharatpur (55 km): Keoladeo bird sanctuary, best from October to February.
  • Chand Baori, Abhaneri: a deep old stepwell, a short detour off the highway.

One long stop, or two short ones, is the right amount. The full detail is in our Agra to Jaipur route guide.

What We Cover, and What We Do Not

We are based in Agra. We can do:

  • Pickup from Agra Cantt station or Agra airport
  • Agra sightseeing, including the Taj Mahal at sunrise
  • The Agra to Jaipur leg, with stops, in either direction
  • The Agra to Delhi Airport leg

We do not operate the direct Jaipur to Delhi leg, because it does not pass through Agra. You would book that with a Jaipur or Delhi operator, or take a train or flight. We also do not sell a fixed all-inclusive Golden Triangle package. Each leg is quoted on its own, and a multi-day plan is quoted once we know your dates.

💡 One car or several?

Keeping one car for the whole circuit is convenient, but you pay for the days it sits idle in each city. Booking leg by leg is often simpler: a local cab in Delhi, us for Agra and the drive to Jaipur, a local cab in Jaipur.

Best Time for the Golden Triangle

October to March. Days are pleasant and the roads are easy. In late December and January, morning fog can slow the Delhi–Agra expressway and hide the Taj at sunrise, so keep a little slack in the plan. April to June is very hot, and sightseeing has to be done early.
