Fatehpur Sikri Guide: What to See, Timings & How to Reach

Akbar's abandoned capital, an hour from Agra. What is free, what needs a ticket, and how to see it without being hassled.

Padma Shree TravelsNovember 20267 min read

Quick Answer — Fatehpur Sikri

From Agra: about 40 km, 1 hour by road

Time needed: 2–3 hours at the site

Open: sunrise to sunset

Tickets: needed for the palace complex only; the mosque side is free

Cab from Agra: ₹2,500 in an AC sedan, driver waits

Fatehpur Sikri was the capital of the Mughal empire for about fourteen years. Akbar built it in the 1570s, and the court left not long after. What remains is a complete red sandstone city on a ridge, empty and very well preserved. It is the best half-day trip from Agra, and it is easy to do badly. This guide is here so you do it well.

The Site Has Two Parts

This is the one thing most first-time visitors do not know, and it changes how you plan.

EntryFreeTicket needed

FeelA living place of worship, busyQuiet, open courtyards
ShoesRemoved at the gateKept on

The two parts are next to each other, a few minutes apart on foot. See both.

What to See

Buland Darwaza

The great gateway, more than 50 metres high from the road. Akbar built it to mark his victory in Gujarat. You climb a long flight of steps to reach it, in the open sun.

Tomb of Salim Chishti

A small white marble tomb in the middle of the mosque courtyard. Akbar built his city here because of this saint. Visitors tie a thread on the marble screens and make a wish.

Diwan-i-Khas

A plain building from outside. Inside is a single carved pillar that holds up a round platform, where Akbar is said to have sat during discussions.

Panch Mahal

A five-storey open pavilion, each floor smaller than the one below. It is the most photographed building on the palace side.

Jodha Bai's Palace and Anup Talao

The largest residence in the complex, and a square pool with a platform at its centre.

Timings and Tickets

The site is open from sunrise to sunset. The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. Fatehpur Sikri is listed as open, which makes it a useful Friday plan, though the mosque is busy around midday prayers that day.

The palace ticket is priced differently for Indian and foreign visitors and is sold at the counter and online. Rates change, so check the board. Tickets are not part of the cab fare.

How to Reach from Agra

Fatehpur Sikri is about 40 km from Agra on the Jaipur highway, roughly an hour by car. Private vehicles stop at the parking area below the ridge. From there a shuttle bus takes visitors up to the monuments for a small fee, or you can walk.

Our Agra to Fatehpur Sikri taxi is ₹2,500 in an AC sedan, with the driver waiting at the parking area. Tolls and parking are extra at actuals.

Guides and Touts

This is the honest part. At the parking area and near Buland Darwaza, many people will offer to guide you, some quite firmly. A few things help:

  • If you want a guide, hire a licensed one and agree the price before you start.
  • If you do not, a clear "no, thank you" and walking on is enough.
  • Offerings at the tomb are your choice. Nobody can require you to buy a cloth or flowers.
  • Keep your shoes with the official shoe-keeper at the gate.

Best Time to Go

October to March is the comfortable season. In any month, go early. By 8:00 AM the light is good and the tour buses have not arrived. In summer, be done by 11:00 AM. There is very little shade on the steps or in the courtyards.

💡 What to carry

Water, a cap, sunglasses and socks. The stone floor of the mosque courtyard gets hot under bare feet, and socks are allowed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fatehpur Sikri is about 40 km from Agra on the Jaipur highway. The drive takes about 1 hour each way by private taxi.

Most visitors need 2 to 3 hours to see both parts of the site: the mosque complex with Buland Darwaza and the Tomb of Salim Chishti, and the palace complex with Diwan-i-Khas and Panch Mahal. With the drive from Agra it is a half-day trip.

The mosque complex, which includes Buland Darwaza, the Jama Masjid and the Tomb of Salim Chishti, is free to enter. The palace complex needs a ticket, priced separately for Indian and foreign visitors. Check current rates at the counter.

Fatehpur Sikri is listed as open from sunrise to sunset, including Fridays, when the Taj Mahal is closed. The mosque area is busy around Friday midday prayers, so visit early in the morning.

A guide is optional. If you want one, hire a licensed guide and agree the price before starting. Many unofficial guides approach visitors at the parking area and the gateway, and it is fine to decline politely.
