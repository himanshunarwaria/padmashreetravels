Agra to Delhi Airport by Road: Route, Time & When to Leave

The drive itself is easy. Leaving at the right time is what decides whether you catch the flight calmly.

Padma Shree TravelsNovember 20266 min read

Quick Answer — Agra to Delhi Airport by Road

Distance: about 230 km

Drive time: 3.5–4 hours, traffic permitting

Route: Yamuna Expressway, then Noida and Delhi roads to IGI

Leave Agra: 5.5–6 hours before an international flight, 4.5–5 hours before a domestic one

Fare: fixed fare confirmed on WhatsApp; tolls at actuals

Many visitors finish their India trip in Agra and fly home from Delhi. The question we hear most is simple: what time should we leave? This guide answers that first, then covers the route, the terminals and a few things that catch people out.

How Far Is Delhi Airport from Agra?

Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI) is about 230 km from Agra. In normal traffic the drive takes 3.5 to 4 hours.

The Route

Almost every car takes the Yamuna Expressway. It runs about 165 km from Agra to Greater Noida, with six lanes and no traffic lights. That part is predictable. The last stretch, through Noida and across Delhi to the airport, is where time is won or lost, especially on weekday evenings.

The expressway has rest areas with washrooms and food courts, so a 15-minute tea break is easy. There are three toll plazas. Tolls are paid at actuals, and we tell you the amount before you book.

When Should You Leave Agra?

Flight typeLeave Agra before departureExample
Domestic4.5–5 hours2:00 PM flight → leave by 9:00–9:30 AM
International5.5–6 hours2:00 AM flight → leave by 8:00–8:30 PM

That covers the drive, a short break, Delhi traffic and the check-in and security lines. Nobody can promise an exact arrival time on a 230 km road, so we always plan with a buffer. Sitting at the gate with a coffee is far better than watching the clock near Noida.

💡 Winter fog

In December and January, fog on the Yamuna Expressway can slow traffic a lot at night and in the early morning. For flights in those weeks, add at least one more hour, or choose a daytime departure from Agra if your flight allows it.

Which Terminal?

IGI has three terminals: T1, T2 and T3. International flights use T3. Domestic flights are split between terminals depending on the airline. The terminals are far apart by road, so check your ticket and send us the terminal number with your flight details. Your driver then goes to the right one the first time.

Taxi or Train to the Airport?

You can take a train to Delhi and then the metro or a local cab to the airport. It is cheaper, and it works if you travel light and your flight is in the daytime. A direct taxi makes more sense when you have heavy bags, children or elderly parents with you, or a late-night flight. It is one vehicle from your hotel door to the terminal door.
